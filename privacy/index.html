Privacy Policy

Kelvara protects onchain positions without holding your keys or your funds. We treat your information the same way: we use what the product needs to work, and nothing more.

In short
  • We never ask for your name, email, seed phrase or private keys to use Kelvara.
  • This website sets no cookies. It counts visits anonymously, without storing anything in your browser.
  • The app uses your wallet address, the exits you sign, session and security data, and anonymous usage analytics.
  • We don't sell your information or use it for advertising.

01Who we are

Kelvara is operated by its founding team. The legal entity is being formed; once it is registered, this policy will name it as the controller of your information. Until then, questions and requests go to contact@kelvara.xyz.

02This website

  • No cookies. kelvara.xyz and its documentation set no cookies and run no advertising trackers. Fonts are served from our own domain.
  • Anonymous analytics. We use PostHog, hosted in the EU, to understand in aggregate how the website is used. It stores nothing on your device, does not record sessions or identify you, and does not keep IP addresses. Your browser's "Do Not Track" setting is respected.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address and browser type to deliver and secure the site, under its own privacy statement.
  • Live slot feed. The homepage shows the current Solana slot by connecting your browser directly to a public Solana RPC endpoint. That operator can see your IP address for the connection.
  • Booking a demo. Demo bookings go through Calendly. What you enter there is handled by Calendly and shared with us so we can hold the meeting.

03The Kelvara app

When you use app.kelvara.xyz, we process:

  • Wallet addresses you connect or check, with the onchain data linked to them, such as positions, token accounts, delegates and transactions. This data is already public on the Solana blockchain, but a wallet address can still identify you.
  • Signed protection transactions. If you enroll in protection, we store the exact transactions you signed in advance, encrypted at rest, so they can be sent when your policy says so. We cannot alter them.
  • Protection settings such as policies, grants, and execution and reconciliation records.
  • Session and security data such as wallet sign-in proofs, session identifiers, IP addresses and request logs, used to authenticate you and keep the service safe.
  • Usage analytics. The app uses PostHog, hosted in the EU, to understand in aggregate how the product is used. Analytics are not linked to your wallet address and do not record sessions. PostHog stores a random identifier in your browser.
  • Browser storage. The app saves the wallet you last used and unfinished enrollment steps in your browser's local storage, so you can pick up where you left off. You can clear it at any time in your browser settings.

We never ask for or receive your seed phrase or private keys. Signing always happens in your own wallet.

04Why we use it

  • To provide the service you asked for: monitoring, alerts, wallet checks and protection. (Legal basis: performance of a contract.)
  • To secure the service, prevent abuse and fix problems. (Legal basis: legitimate interests.)
  • To understand how the website and app are used and improve them. (Legal basis: legitimate interests, or your consent where the law requires it.)
  • To answer you when you contact us. (Legal basis: legitimate interests.)
  • To meet legal obligations where they apply.

We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

05Who we share it with

  • Infrastructure providers that host and run the service, such as cloud hosting, storage, Solana RPC and analytics (PostHog) providers, acting on our instructions.
  • The Solana network. Transactions we submit on your behalf become public onchain, as with any Solana transaction.
  • Authorities where the law requires it.

Some providers may process data outside your country. Where that happens, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

06How long we keep it

We keep account and protection data while you use the service. Signed transactions are kept until they are used, revoked or invalidated. Execution records are kept as long as needed to evidence what happened. Logs and analytics are kept for a limited period. When data is no longer needed, we delete or anonymize it. We cannot delete data recorded on the blockchain.

07Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to access, correct, delete, restrict or port your information, and you can object to how we use it. To make a request, email contact@kelvara.xyz. You can also complain to your local data protection authority; in Poland, that is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO).

08Children

Kelvara is not intended for anyone under 18.

09Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. For significant changes, we will give notice in the app.

10Contact
