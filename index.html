Sign the exit once.

You sign it ahead of time: a full withdrawal to your own wallet, in three priority-fee variants. Durable nonces keep them valid until they're needed. Kelvara stores the signed bytes. It can send them, but it can't change a single one.

Kelvara

Then Kelvara keeps watch.

It follows the accounts that can change your position: the program's upgrade authority, the Squads multisig behind it, its members, threshold and proposals, and unusual transaction activity. Every reading is stored with its slot and source. A reading that goes stale shows as unknown, never as safe.

Someone with the keys

At 3 a.m., a new admin takes the keys.

Since launch, the vault's code could only change with the team multisig's approval. Tonight that power moves to one wallet nobody announced, and one key can rewrite the vault. The timelock on this vault is 0: a change takes effect the moment it lands, so nobody gets a window to react. Kelvara sees the change once it is final on the blockchain, alerts you and steps in as your rules say.

Much of DeFi's capital still has no one on watch.

01

We hold your exit, never your keys.

Kelvara holds one transaction you signed: a full withdrawal to your own wallet. It can send it. It can't change a byte, send funds anywhere else or act on anything you didn't sign. No custody, no trading mandate, no delegated authority.

02

Decided on Tuesday. Done at 3 a.m.

An in-house team still needs its signers awake to approve a withdrawal. With Kelvara, your committee approves the exit in advance. When your rules say go, it runs without waiting for anyone.

03

We don't wait to prove it's a hack.

You deposited when a team multisig controlled the vault. If that changes, Kelvara steps you out, no forensics needed. The vaults are permissionless, so you can deposit again once control is restored. A false alarm costs a short time outside. A real one costs everything.

Kelvara works alongside your security team. They set the rules. Kelvara carries them out at any hour.

Legal & compliance

No custody. Clear terms.

01

Non-custodial

Kelvara never receives your seed phrase or private keys and never holds your funds. The exit you sign can only pay your own wallet.

02

Not advice or insurance

Kelvara is a monitoring and execution tool. It is not investment advice, an audit, insurance or a guarantee against loss.

03

Minimal data

No cookies, no ads, no tracking across sites. We count visits anonymously, and the app uses only what it needs: your wallet address, the exits you sign and your session.

04

Explicit coverage

Kelvara protects supported positions only, today three Kamino vaults on Solana mainnet. An exit lands only if the protocol and Solana accept it.
