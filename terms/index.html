02What Kelvara does

For supported positions, Kelvara:

  • reads public onchain data about your wallet and positions;
  • monitors the accounts that can change a position, such as program upgrade authorities, Squads multisigs, their members, thresholds and proposals, and unusual transaction activity;
  • alerts you and shows the evidence behind each reading, with its slot and source;
  • if you enroll in protection, stores the withdrawal transactions you signed in advance and submits one of them when the policy you approved says so.

The supported positions are listed in the documentation. Coverage differs by position and capability, and we may add or remove positions.

03Non-custodial by design

  • Your keys stay with you. Kelvara never asks for or receives your seed phrase or private keys. Never share them with anyone claiming to be Kelvara.
  • Your funds stay with you. Kelvara does not hold, pool or move funds on its own account.
  • Exact transactions only. A pre-signed exit fixes its destination, accounts, instructions, amounts, signer set and fee policy. Kelvara can submit it but cannot change it; any change would invalidate your signature. It pays only your own wallet.
  • Signing in is not consent to transact. Signing the login message proves you control a wallet. It does not authorize any transaction.
  • You can withdraw authorization. You can invalidate unused pre-signed exits by advancing or reclaiming their durable nonce accounts, as described in the docs.

07Risks and limits

DeFi is risky and you can lose some or all of your funds. In particular:

  • Kelvara cannot predict every exploit, key compromise or protocol failure, and not every risk shows up as a change of control.
  • Monitoring cannot guarantee that there is enough time or liquidity to exit.
  • Execution depends on the protocol, on Solana and on RPC providers accepting and confirming the transaction. Congestion, fees, outages, program upgrades or vault rules can delay or block an exit.
  • A reading that goes stale is shown as unknown, never as safe. Kelvara may pause automatic sending when evidence is missing or a check fails, which means an exit may not be sent.
  • Protection may trigger when nothing malicious is happening. A false alarm can leave your funds outside the vault for a while and may cost fees or missed yield.
